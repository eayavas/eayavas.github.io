# who am i?
|\__/,| (`\ _.|o o |_ ) ) -(((---(((--------

  I am a multidisciplinary researcher and creative technologist. While my academic roots are in computer engineering, my primary focus has shifted toward new media arts, algorithmic audiovisuals, and interactive installations.

  I am also a dedicated advocate for free and open-source software, digital privacy, and hacker ethics. To support these principles, I prioritize decentralized platforms like the Fediverse over centralized networks and actively self-host my media and working environments. I believe that true creative expression requires a strong foundation in digital rights and privacy-respecting technologies.
