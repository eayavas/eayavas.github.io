# About Me

  I am a multidisciplinary researcher and creative technologist. While my academic roots are in computer engineering, my primary focus has shifted toward new media arts, algorithmic audiovisuals, and interactive installations.

  I am a dedicated advocate for free and open-source software, digital privacy, and hacker ethics. To support these principles, I prioritize decentralized platforms like the Fediverse over centralized networks and actively self-host my media and working environments. I believe that true creative expression requires a strong foundation in digital rights and privacy-respecting technologies.

# Research & Practise

  My creative output and ongoing research span across software, visual arts, and physical fabrication. My current domains of focus include:

  • Livecoding: Algorithmic music performance and real-time audio synthesis.
    Tools: Supercollider, FoxDot, TidalCycles, Strudel
  • DSP & Music Production: Digital signal processing, sound design, and electronic music composition. Tools: Tenacity, Ardour & Mostly FOSS plugins
  • Interactive Visuals: Generative coding, audio-reactive systems, and real-time graphics.
    Tools: openFramework, OpenCV, Hydra Synth
  • Photography & Cinematography: Mostly street photography and visual documentation. Tools: Gimp, Darktable, Fujifilm XT30, Samsung NX300
  • 3D Printed Artworks: Parametric modeling with OpenSCAD and digital fabrication of physical art pieces. Tools: openSCAD, BambuLab A1
